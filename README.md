# Quake 4 Survivor Mod

## Accessing the mode

To begin playing the mod, you must type *survivorMode* into the console. Once the mode begins, the following will occur:

**Survivor Mode:**
- Enemies spawn in a radius around the player.
- More enemies and new types will spawn depending on the time survived.
- Defeat enemies to gain XP.
- The player levels up once the exp bar hits 100%.
- Upon leveling up, the player can select a new weapon or buff to gain.

**In total, there are 13 different level up rewards, those being:**
- Blaster
- Nailgun
- Grenade Launcher
- Railgun
- Rocket Launcher
- Napalm Gun
- Lightning Gun
- Dark Matter Gun
- Shotgun
- Machinegun
- Haste (Q4 Multiplayer effect)
- Ammo Regen (Q4 Multiplayer effect)
- Quad Damage (Q4 Multiplayer effect)

## Weapon Changes

**Blaster:**

The blaster now has a homing effect. Additionally, the longer the player charges the weapon more projectiles will be added.

**Nailgun:**

The nailgun now fires in a three-round burst instead of having the original machine gun shooting style.

**Grenade Launcher:**

The grenade launcher now also fires in a burst pattern, firing five grenades one after the other. Grenades also now bounce and explode on every bounce.

**Railgun:**

The railgun now does little to no damage and instead causes enemies to be "frozen" on hit for a few seconds.

**Napalm Gun:**

The napalm gun fires a projectile that attaches to the player, moving along with them. The projectile deals damage over time to enemies within its radius.

**Shotgun:**

The shotgun now fires in a spread shot effect, firing projectiles at specific angles. Additionally, instead of the normal shotgun projectiles, the shotgun now fires the Dark Matter Gun's projectiles.

**Machinegun:**

The machine gun now fires in a spread shot and burst pattern. The gun fires seven projectiles in a five-round burst.

**Dark Matter Gun:**

The DMG now fires a single bouncing projectile that tracks and homes in on enemies.

**Rocket Launcher:** 

The rocket launcher now fires a normal rocket projectile that explodes into multiple DMG projectiles on impact.

**Lightning Gun:**

The lightning gun now fires a single beam that begins to sway left and right the longer the player holds the attack. Additionally, the chain lightning effect is now fired from directly above the enemy, making it look like a lightning strike.
